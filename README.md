**CodePulse X** is an elite, industrial-grade cognitive development lab for developers. It measures typing speed, code accuracy, and mental resilience under tactical stress in a cinematic, high-performance environment.

---

## 🕹️ Game Dynamics

- **🏎️ Speed Test**: Measure raw WPM and accuracy across 10+ programming languages.
- **🐞 Syntax Fixer**: A specialized mode where you must debug injected errors on the fly.
- **🙈 Blind Mode**: Silent error tracking — your mistakes are only revealed at the end.
- **💥 Glitch Mode**: Random UI distortion and spelling swaps to test focus under pressure.
- **🏆 Boss Fight**: A 120-second gauntlet combining all modes with increasing difficulty.

## 🤖 AI Diagnosis

At the end of every session, your raw metrics (WPM, Accuracy, Consistency) are analyzed by the **Gemini model**. It doesn't just give you a score; it provides a **Cognitive Performance Audit**, identifying whether your bottlenecks are syntax recognition, muscle memory, or focus stability.
